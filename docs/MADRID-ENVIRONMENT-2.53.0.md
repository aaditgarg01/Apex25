# Madrid environment refresh

The 5,436.345 m playable centreline and its original elevation samples remain
in use. The promoter documents a 5,474 m design and the FIA competition map
documents 5,414 m with auxiliary labels 5A and 20A. The environment follows
the game route and the 22 principal turn labels.

## Runoff design

Dimensions are target metres beyond the racing edge on the listed dominant
side. Widths taper at ends and are clipped against the racing ribbon, pit
lane and mapped access routes. The opposite side and intervening straights
use a base asphalt/grass/concrete stack with the same clearance checks. Each
turn gets an edge stripe and a fence or nearby boundary. Access routes and
verges continue beyond the walls where there is room. Gravel width and depth
are zero throughout this plan. A documented escape verifies the paved type
and location, with the width still fitted to the game.

## Verification

- Full footprints for 542 placed scenery sites were checked against 28,419
  road polygons. Minimum conservative footprint clearances were 4.51 m to
  the race ribbon, 2.04 m to the pit lane and 0.77 m to a mapped access route.
  The nearest hall cleared all roads by 1.83 m. Street furniture cleared all
  roads by 0.89 m. Minimum bridge/gantry pier clearances were 9.46 m to
  race, 1.37 m to pit and 0.38 m to access. Overhead decks cleared race and
  pit by 7.80 m and access by 5.00 m at sampled crossings.
- All 9,630 generated runoff and kerb polygons cleared the road and building
  footprints. The 22 turns had classified kerb, painted edge, paved runoff,
  grass and wall coverage. The mesh terrain cleared sampled race and pit
  surfaces by 0.34 m and 0.37 m respectively. The saved elevation range is
  30.03 m. The largest adjacent elevation step is 0.281 m.
- All 7,119 rendered wall, fence, pier and overhead records matched the
  collision records. Maximum mesh vertex deviation was 0.14 mm. Static
  contact probes reached every record. The La Monumental outer wall had 364
  checked endpoints and a maximum 0.053 m height difference to the adjacent
  verge edge across the 1 m wall setback. Madrid roll and road camber
  remained zero across 3,895 samples through the corner. Another 414
  on-track driving states matched the prior physics step in dry,
  intermediate and wet conditions.
- Pit probes confirmed no early limiter, a valid late entry and an exit after
  service. The lane remains 558 m and the computed time loss was 18.005 s
  excluding service. The lane's station, taper, width and service location
  match the prior revision. All 41 other pit plans were identical.
- Scenery uses 8,126 instanced spectators, 226 trees, 88 scrub clusters, 50
  flags, 9 tents, 9 support vehicles, 28 marshal lights and 3 pooled flare
  sprites. Static scenery had 209 mesh submissions across the whole venue
  after batching. Parked browser views showed 87–147 draw calls on the pit
  straight, La Monumental and two wetness levels. The console reported no
  errors or warnings during those views.
- Syntax, undefined names and shadowing, module imports, and both build
  commands were checked. The game's test suite was untouched and was
  outside the requested run.

## Reference gaps

Exact temporary stand positions and dimensions, wall setbacks, kerb
profiles, the outlines of the new garage extensions and the deck height of
mapped bridges could not be established from the available maps. Hall
footprints were reduced or shifted where a mapped road or other reserved
area required it. The retained road profile differs from 25 nearby DEM
spot samples by 2.07 m RMS and up to 6.17 m. The DEM includes surrounding
terrain and structure effects and was used for scenery only. No Madrid
attachments were present with this request. The design used the official
sources and public engineering photos listed in [Track sources](../TRACK-SOURCES.md).
