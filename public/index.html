        The guide follows a cornering line. Green: accelerate. Amber: lift. Red: brake for your approach speed and surface conditions. Qualifying lasts seven minutes, starting with one ghosted outlap. Cross the line to begin each flying lap; your best valid time sets the grid. Finish an invalid lap on track before trying again. A flying lap started before the clock expires may be completed afterward. Recovery and lap restarts are unavailable in qualifying. Steering is fully manual.

        Choose Normal / dry, Intermediate / damp, or Wet / rain before starting. Rain reduces traction and braking grip, increases sliding and makes kerbs slippery. Brake earlier and use gentler throttle in wet conditions. Use Randomise beside either selector to choose another circuit or surface at random before starting. The host chooses the circuit and conditions for the whole online room.

        Gamepad: left stick, RT accelerate, LT brake, A boost. Touch controls appear on mobile.
